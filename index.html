From me, to the girl I cannot stop choosing

Happy 21st Birthday, my love.

This is a tiny website for the girl with the softest smile, the most dangerous side-eye, the cutest chaos, and the kind of heart that makes me feel lucky every day.

Open your wish
A small piece of my heart, wrapped as a page
21 looks unfairly beautiful on you

I hope this year is gentle with your heart and loud with your happiness.

I hope you never forget how loved you are. Not just on your birthday, not just when you are smiling in pictures, but on the tired days, the overthinking days, the messy-hair days, and every normal day in between.

A little letter

To my favorite person

I love the way you smile like you are trying not to smile too much. I love the way you look at me like you already know what nonsense I am about to say. I love your drama, your softness, your stubborn little moods, and the way you become home without even trying.

These pictures are not just pictures to me. They are proof of beach nights, mirror selfies, sleepy smiles, silly faces, tight hugs, and a hundred tiny moments where I looked at you and thought, yes, this is my person.

On your 21st birthday, I just want you to know this: I am proud of you, I am lucky to love you, and I cannot wait to see every version of you that this new year brings.

Always yours, even when I am annoying.

Our tiny universe

My memories of Ishani

The places that became ours because you were there.

01

Beach dates

I do not even know how many times we went to the beach. At first, I never understood what was so exciting about it. But now, beaches without you feel incomplete. I do not think I could ever go there alone without longing for you.

You, me, our bike, and my earphones saw us slowly fall in love. Shangumugham, Kovalam, and our everyday St. Andrews beach will always hold pieces of us.

02

Chai Time

We walked to Chai Time so many times, but it was never really about coffee. It was about the walk. In the beginning, you would not even hold my whole hand, and you said you would not get on the bike either.

Somehow, I got both. A huge win for me. And all those silent walks where we barely spoke still meant everything. That silence was beautiful because it was ours.

03

Your hostel entrance

That little aisle saw us kiss for the first time. After that, I almost could not pass it without wanting to hug you, kiss you, or at least hope I would see you.

I do not know how I will pass that road now without looking for you there. Some places become ordinary for everyone else, but for me, that entrance will always have you in it.

04

My room

Sorry, Jeevan was always there. And now he has gone home, but you are not here either. It makes me miss the days when my room became the best place in the world just because you were in it. We planned to do so many things and somehow ended up doing nothing except being close, kissing, laughing, and staying wrapped up in each other.

The cries, the hugs, the affirmations, the comfort, the chaos, and obviously the biscuits. I will miss all of it so much, Ishani.

05

And finally, you

You are the best person for me, Ishani. I cannot imagine someone else in your place. You make my day every day, and you push me to keep living forward even when everything feels heavy.

You are a beautiful, brilliant human being who loves people with so much heart. I do not know how I will survive here without you, because college was you, Trivandrum was you, and so much of my life here became meaningful because of you. Everything feels incomplete without you.

My promise

I will keep choosing you, especially in the little ways.

In the birthday moments, the normal days, the confused days, the excited days, when you are glowing, when you are tired, when you are mad at me for something I probably did, and in all the days still waiting for us.

Happy 21st, my love. You are my favorite everything.
